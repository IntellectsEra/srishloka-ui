Sri Shloka should feel like:
A premium Indian floral marketplace in America — not an Indian grocery website.

Premium American e-commerce
Indian cultural sophistication
Freshness / botanical aesthetics
Editorial photography

Modern typography

Clean, spacious layouts

AI-looking gradients

41. The Brand Experience in One Sentence
I would design the entire website around this feeling:
"I found exactly what I was looking for — and Sri Shloka feels like a brand I can trust."
Not:
"Wow, this website has lots of animations."
That distinction is important.


My recommended creative direction
If I were leading the redesign, I would not copy AJIO.
I would borrow the commerce UX principles from AJIO/Nordstrom/modern DTC brands, but give Sri Shloka its own visual identity.
The formula would be:
Premium American commerce UX
Indian botanical visual language
Editorial photography
Very clean navigation
Exceptional product discovery
Strong B2B infrastructure
= Sri Shloka Flowers
The key is to make the shopping experience feel world-class while the brand personality feels unmistakably Sri Shloka.
One strategic change I'd make immediately
Make SHOP and WHOLESALE the two dominant actions throughout the website.
Everything else — Story, Locations, Mission, Values — should support those two journeys rather than compete with them.
That will give you the simple, straight-forward, premium $1M-brand feel you're looking for without turning the site into an over-engineered design showcase.
